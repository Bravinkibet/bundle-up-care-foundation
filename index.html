For the smallest of us, and the women raising them

A baby doesn’t stay a newborn for long, and neither do the clothes that fit them. As children grow through their first five years, their needs change quickly, and keeping up with clothes, shoes, and other essentials can be a challenge for many families.

Bundle Up Care Foundation exists to help bridge that gap. We gather items children have outgrown and give them a new purpose, sharing them with families who can make use of them, so that every child can have the comfort, care, and essentials they need as they grow.

We accept whatever you can give

Every contribution matters. Whether it’s something your child has outgrown, something new, or something that has been waiting to be useful again, your gift can bring comfort, joy, and support to another family.

Accepted

Clothes

Onesies, sleepsuits, jumpers, dresses, and everyday wear for babies and children aged 0–5. Every piece shared is a thoughtful gift that can make a child feel comfortable and cared for.

Accepted

Shoes

Children’s feet grow quickly. Shoes for crawling, first steps, and beyond are welcome, helping little feet stay comfortable as they grow.

Accepted

Toys

Comfort and play are important too. Toys suitable for children aged 0–5 can bring smiles, spark imagination, and become a special part of another child’s day.

How a bundle finds its way home

From a well-wisher’s home to a child and family who can benefit from it, here’s the journey every donation takes.

You give

A well-wisher sets aside clothes, shoes, or toys their little one has outgrown, or chooses something new to donate and share with a child in need.

We collect

Donations are collected through drop-off points, community collection drives, or sent directly to us by post.

We sort

Every item is carefully checked, cleaned when needed, and sorted by age and season into thoughtfully prepared bundles, ready to be given to families.

We deliver

Bundles are delivered directly to families at home, making it easier for them to receive the items they need with dignity, convenience, and care.

Bundle Up Care Foundation collects clothes, shoes, and toys from well-wishers and shares them directly with families raising children aged 0–5. Through these thoughtful donations, we provide practical support to children and families, helping ensure that every child feels cared for, valued, and supported.

Children quickly outgrow clothes, shoes, and toys, while many gently used items remain unused in homes where they are no longer needed. The foundation was created to bridge that gap by connecting these useful items with families who can benefit from them, creating a simple way for people to share, support, and make a meaningful difference.

It starts small, but it is built to grow, bundle by bundle, family by family.

Maureen Chebet
Founder & Owner, Bundle Up Care Foundation

Take part in whatever way fits you

Bundle Up Care grows through the generosity of well-wishers and the time, effort, and care they choose to share. There is a place for everyone who wants to be part of making a difference.

1Donate clothes

Pass on baby and toddler clothing your child has outgrown, or choose something new for a bundle. Every size and every season is welcome.

2Help with shipping

Donations sometimes need help getting from a well-wisher’s doorstep to us, or from us to the families we serve. If you can help deliver a parcel, you can make a difference.

3Sort and pack

Join a sorting session by checking, folding, and packing donations into thoughtful bundles ready for delivery.

4Spread the word

Tell a friend, share a collection drive, or connect us with a family who could benefit from a bundle. Every conversation helps more people discover how they can take part.
